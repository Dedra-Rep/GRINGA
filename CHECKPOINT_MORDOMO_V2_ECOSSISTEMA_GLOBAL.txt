CHECKPOINT_MORDOMO_V2_ECOSSISTEMA_GLOBAL

This checkpoint marks the successful transition of Mordomo.AI from a personal shopper / simple utility paradigm into a high-level, 30-year vision "Intelligent Care System" with a global footprint.

Key Architecture and UI changes implemented:
1. Removed all traditional Multi-Level Marketing or affiliate network concepts.
2. Created the "Mordomo Trust Circle" (Círculo de Confiança Mordomo) recognition tiers.
3. Designed the executive-level "Tranquility Index" (Índice de Tranquilidade) for life dashboard visualization.
4. Integrated "Weekly Missions" (Missões da Semana), "Shared Life" (Vida Compartilhada), and "Trust Marketplace" (Marketplace de Confiança).
5. Highlighted the core global metric: "Hours of tranquility returned to people" (Horas de tranquilidade devolvidas às pessoas).
6. Protected all secure backend and auth code.
